# Contributor licence agreement (proposal)

**Status: proposed, not in force.** Until it is adopted, pull requests from outside contributors are not accepted.

## Why one is needed

The engine is dual-licensed by side: app-side crates under MIT or Apache 2.0, server-side crates under AGPL 3.0 only (see the README). The Product Line Plan keeps the right to relicense later, and to run the server in the hosted plans without publishing every change. Both depend on holding the rights to all of the code. An outside contribution under the inbound licence alone would not grant that.

## What it would grant

Each outside contributor, once, before their first pull request is merged:

- **A copyright licence** to Orbyn to use, modify, distribute and sublicense the contribution under any licence, including the current ones and any future licence of the project.
- **A patent licence** for the contribution, as in Apache 2.0, section 3.
- **A statement** that the contributor has the right to grant both, for example that an employer does not own the work.
- **The contributor keeps their copyright.** This is a licence, not an assignment.

## How it would work

- A CLA bot on the repository asks each new contributor to sign once, with a click, and records the signature.
- The CLA text is published in this directory beside this proposal, and versioned.
- Commits from the owner's account and from bots are exempt.

## Open before adoption

- **The text itself.** A lawyer drafts or reviews it, together with the licence choices, as the Product Line Plan requires.
- **The signing tool.**
- **The beneficiary.** The licence files name Tran Duc Anh Dang as the copyright holder. If Orbyn becomes a legal entity, the CLA and the copyright lines must name the same holder.
- **Scope.** Whether the same CLA also covers orbyn-planner and the other Orbyn repositories.
